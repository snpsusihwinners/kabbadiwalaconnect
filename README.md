## About Kabaddi

Kabaddi is a contact team sport originating from ancient India. Two teams of seven players each take turns sending a "raider" into the opposing half to tag out defenders and return safely, all while holding their breath (called a "raid"). The sport is one of the most popular in South Asia and has gained international recognition, with professional leagues like the Pro Kabaddi League (PKL) and inclusion in the Asian Games. Known for its combination of agility, strength, and strategy, Kabaddi is often called the "game of the brave."
